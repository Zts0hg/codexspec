### Con-2026-0812-2114vj-1: Editing a distributed command's frontmatter description/argument-hint requires updating the translation catalogs

- claim: When you change the `description` or `argument-hint` frontmatter of a distributed command, you MUST update `templates/translations/en.json` to match the template (or `test_en_translation_catalog_matches_template_frontmatter` fails on drift), and update the 7 localized JSONs (`de/es/fr/ja/ko/pt-BR/zh-CN`) in lockstep with translated text — all 9 catalogs carry identical top-level keys. **Policy change (2026-09-01, full localization)**: the former subset precedent ("brand-new commands like debug/distill/evolve/config install with English frontmatter") is RETIRED — every distributed command must have a catalog entry, enforced by `TestFullCatalogCoverage` in `tests/test_translation_files.py`; a new command adds its catalog entry in the same change (the old S7.1 "release-notes installs uncataloged" guard test was flipped accordingly). Localization rule: pure-syntax hints with nothing translatable (e.g. `debug`'s token list, `onboard`'s `[path]` → localized bracket label) may keep their tokens verbatim; any hint carrying explanatory prose must be localized per language.
